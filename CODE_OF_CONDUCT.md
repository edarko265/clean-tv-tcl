# Code of Conduct

This project builds on a community that helps people take control of their own televisions. Keep it
welcoming, patient, safety-conscious, and respectful.

## Expected

- Be kind. Many contributors are doing this on a parent's or grandparent's TV, in a second
  language, on their first pull request.
- Assume good faith. A list that breaks something was tested on different firmware, not written to
  hurt anyone.
- Say what you tested on. Specifics ("Android TV 11, build RTT1, Hisense A4") help; verdicts
  ("doesn't work") do not.
- Respect the maintainers' decisions on scope. The launcher stays small, offline and without
  prebuilt binaries; if your idea does not fit, a fork with a SHOWCASE row is a good outcome, not a
  rejection of you.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks, in issues,
  pull requests, Discussions or the showcase.
- Publishing other people's private information: device identifiers, Wi-Fi names, IP addresses,
  ADB keys or anything that could identify a TV or a home.
- Lists or instructions that uninstall, root, or intentionally damage a TV, or that target a
  specific person's device.
- Spam, affiliate links, or passing off someone else's work as your own.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and block accounts that break these rules, with a
short explanation. Report ordinary conduct concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. If a
report contains a vulnerability or private device information, use the confidential process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

This is a short, plain-language code adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), and
from the upstream CleanYourTV community guidelines.
