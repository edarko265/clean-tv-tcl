# Theme library wallpaper credits

The theme gallery's configuration files are covered by this repository's MIT licence. Wallpapers have
the separate provenance and licences listed below.

## Original AI-assisted illustrations

The following wallpapers were generated for this fork with OpenAI's built-in image generator from
text-only prompts. No reference image, existing character, film, game, club crest or other franchise design
was supplied. They were reviewed, cropped to 16:9 where needed and released under CC0 1.0, as far as the
generated pictures carry any rights to release:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- `araxda-cyber/wallpaper.jpg`: Araxda-inspired plum and violet cyber-engineering studio with
  chips, microcontrollers, connected systems and original robots.
- `ai-frontier/wallpaper.jpg`: original robotics, agent networks and computational intelligence laboratory.
- `blockbuster-spectacle/wallpaper.jpg`: original large-scale cinematic adventure with no named film.
- `football-night/wallpaper.jpg`: floodlit football stadium, rain and tactical energy with no club branding.
- `tactical-extraction/wallpaper.jpg`: original covert extraction-zone atmosphere with no game branding.
- `ghana-africa/wallpaper.jpg`: contemporary Accra, African engineering and Ghana-inspired colour.
- `malta/wallpaper.jpg`: Valletta harbour architecture at blue hour.
- `wasteland/wallpaper.jpg`: post-storm solar engineering and resilient settlement.
- `zombie-apocalypse/wallpaper.jpg`: non-gory survival dawn in an abandoned city.
- `nordic-aurora/wallpaper.jpg`: Finnish winter landscape, aurora and modern lakeside cabin.
- `abyssal-lab/wallpaper.jpg`: deep-ocean research station, bioluminescence and original robotics.
- `orbital-foundry/wallpaper.jpg`: orbital construction yard and original exploration vessel.
- `neon-arcade/wallpaper.jpg`: unbranded retro-futurist arcade with original screen graphics.
- `bioelectric-jungle/wallpaper.jpg`: bioluminescent rainforest and an original monitoring rover.
